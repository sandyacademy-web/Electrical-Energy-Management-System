Report Functions

Nine Management Reporting Functions

The Management Report module transforms information from each EEMS module into structured management information for monitoring, review and decision-making.

MR-01

Executive Dashboard

Consolidated view of critical system performance, status, exceptions and management priorities.

MR-02

Electrical Performance

Electrical availability, load, power quality, protection and system condition.

MR-03

Energy Performance

Energy consumption, demand, efficiency, intensity, cost and savings performance.

MR-04

Maintenance Performance

Asset availability, maintenance backlog, work orders, reliability and completion status.

MR-05

HSE Performance

Incident, near miss, risk, inspection, corrective action and safety performance.

MR-06

Project Performance

Project progress, schedule, cost, risk, quality, commissioning and closeout.

MR-07

Financial Performance

Budget, actual cost, commitment, variance, energy cost and financial control.

MR-08

HR & Competency

Manpower, competency, training, authorization and personnel performance.

MR-09

Action & Improvement Tracking

Corrective actions, improvement programs, ownership, deadlines and closure status.

Management Control

What is Reported?

Electrical Status
Availability, load, power quality, protection, condition and electrical incidents.
Energy Performance
Consumption, demand, cost, efficiency, intensity, variance and savings.
Maintenance
Asset availability, preventive maintenance, corrective work, backlog and reliability.
HSE / K3
Risk, permit, inspection, incident, near miss, training and corrective action.
Project
Scope, schedule, progress, cost, risk, quality, commissioning and handover.
Finance
Budget, actual, commitment, variance, procurement and energy-related cost.
Human Resources
Manpower, competency, qualification, authorization, training and performance.
Corrective Actions
Open actions, ownership, due date, priority and closure effectiveness.
Improvement Program
Improvement initiatives, expected benefits, implementation status and realized results.

Reporting Principles

Information That Management Can Trust

Accurate Information must be based on valid, verified and traceable data.
Timely Information must reach management at the right time for action.
Integrated Different EEMS modules must provide one consistent management picture.
Actionable Every significant exception should lead toward a decision or action.

See the System. Make the Decision.

Management reporting is not the end of the system. It is the bridge from information to improvement.
